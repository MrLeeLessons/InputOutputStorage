💡 Hardware means the physical equipment you can touch. Software means the programs that run on a computer, such as a web browser or word processor. Some devices can perform more than one role; in this activity each device is classified by its main purpose.

☁️ Extension: Cloud storage

Cloud storage saves files on computers (servers) accessed over a network rather than only on a device beside you.

Advantages

Access files from different connected devices and share them with others. Cloud services may also keep backup copies.

Disadvantages

You often need internet access, and files are held by another provider. Accounts and access permissions must be protected.
